The Youth Alive Fellowship (YAF) is the youth arm of the Winners Chapel International Church Worldwide. We are driven to Raise a Generation Alive in Spirit, Soul and Body through Spiritual, Social and total development of our young adults, empowering and equipping them to impact the World.
